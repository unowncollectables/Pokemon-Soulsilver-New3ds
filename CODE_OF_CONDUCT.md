# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive
- giving and accepting helpful feedback
- focusing on technical improvement and collaboration
- being patient with newcomers and different experience levels
- acknowledging and correcting mistakes when they are made

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- discriminatory or offensive language
- public or private harassment
- unwanted sexual attention or advances
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a technical community

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at the repository owner or via a private contact channel if one is established.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
